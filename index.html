05 — SEO Content Writing

Amaly Natural Store — Gulf & Global Market

Content Strategy
Article Architecture That Ranks

Developed long-form SEO articles (2000+ words) for Amaly Store — an essential oils, perfume, and massage oil brand targeting Gulf and global audiences. Every article was structured with H1/H2/H4 headings and a table of contents designed to trigger SERP site link extensions, with keyword density maintained at 1.5% for maximum ranking potential without keyword stuffing.

Audience & Execution
Natural Voice, Real Rankings

Target audience spanned adults, patients using essential oils therapeutically, beauty and medical business owners, natural product enthusiasts, mothers using oils for children, and athletes with joint pain. All content was written to sound naturally human — not keyword-heavy, not AI-generated — to meet both user expectations and search engine quality standards.

SEO Guidelines Applied: 2000+ word minimum · H1/H2/H4 structure · Table of contents for SERP site links · 1.5% keyword density · Natural human tone · On-page SEO throughout
